# The Fall of Lehman Brothers: Financial Ratio Analysis

Individual assignment for DA3411 Business Valuation and Analysis,
University of Moratuwa.

## Objective
Analyze Lehman Brothers' 2006 and 2007 annual reports and 2008 Q2
interim report to find the financial causes of its 2008 collapse.

## Methods
- Profitability: ROE, net profit margin
- Asset quality: risk asset ratio, derivatives-to-equity
- Liquidity: current ratio, quick ratio
- Solvency: debt to equity, debt to capital, interest coverage

## Key Findings
- Leverage peaked at 30.7x in 2007.
- Net income fell from $3.9B (2007) to a $2.4B loss (Q2 2008).
- Current ratio stayed below 0.5 across the period.
- Interest coverage dropped below 1.0 in 2008.
